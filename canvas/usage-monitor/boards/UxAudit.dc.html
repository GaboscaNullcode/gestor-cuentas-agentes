Usage Monitor redesign: what changes and why

The panel answers one question in a glance: which account can I keep working on, and when does the blocked one come back. Today it shows the raw data but leaves that reading to the user.

The visual system

Dark first, both themes

A tray utility is glanced at next to editors and terminals. Graphite neutrals with a slight cool tint; the light theme swaps only the neutrals and follows the OS.

Geist and Geist Mono

Mono with tabular numbers for every percentage and path, so values do not jitter when they update every few minutes.

Color means level, nothing else

Green, amber and red share lightness and chroma in oklch. Providers get a quiet tint on a generic mark, never a competing color.

How to read a usage bar

Fill% of the window used, colored by level
Pace tick% of the window's time already elapsed
Fill past the tickyou will hit the limit before the reset at this rate
The window stays 380×620: the header and the Most headroom strip stay fixed and the cards scroll under them, so the answer to where to switch is always visible.
